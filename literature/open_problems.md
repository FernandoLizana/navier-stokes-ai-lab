# Open Problems (Clay-adjacent)

1. Global regularity vs finite-time singularity for 3D NS, Routes A–D (Clay).
2. Uniqueness of Leray–Hopf weak solutions in 3D.
3. Structure of possible singularities (Type I vs II) if any exist.
4. Whether geometric vorticity alignment can be proved universally.
5. Whether computer-assisted a posteriori methods can certify long-time 3D orbits at Clay-relevant generality.
6. Transfer of boundary/Euler singularity constructions to periodic NS with \(\nu>0\) (generally blocked without new ideas).
