# Known Results (primary, accepted)

- Global weak Leray–Hopf solutions exist; uniqueness/smoothness open in 3D (Leray, Hopf).
- 2D global unique smooth solutions (Ladyzhenskaya).
- Partial regularity for suitable weak solutions: singular set 1-dimensional in space-time (CKN).
- Conditional regularity: Prodi–Serrin, Escauriaza–Šeregin–Šverák endpoint \(L^3\).
- Beale–Kato–Majda continuation via \(\int\|\omega\|_\infty\).
- Small-data global regularity in critical spaces (Kato, Koch–Tataru, Besov frameworks).
- Nonexistence of certain self-similar singularity profiles under decay (Nečas–Růžička–Šverák, Tsai, Chae et al.).
- Weak non-uniqueness via convex integration (Buckmaster–Vicol) — **not** smooth blow-up.
- Instantaneous analyticity / Gevrey for NS on short times (Foias–Temam).
