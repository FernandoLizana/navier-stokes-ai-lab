# Claimed Solutions (watchlist)

Entries classified `claimed_solutions` in `literature_matrix.csv` aggregate arXiv/preprint claims of full Clay resolution.

**NS-MRL policy:** treat as **unverified** until peer review, corrections, and community reception are checked. Preprints are never N11. Incompatible claims (regularity + blow-up for the same official hypotheses) are flagged by `python/ns_exploration/conjectures/claim_conflict.py`.
